What it is

Six instruments, not six dashboards

You drop a ZIP of paintings onto the deck of a ship called The Warden. The archive is stored, embedded and analysed by six machine-learning and computer-vision pipelines — and handed back as six places you can walk into.

Type “a stormy sea at night” and the collection re-forms as a constellation. Open the logbook and an unnamed canvas gets a style, a genre and an artist. Spread the chart table and five centuries of painting lay themselves out by visual kinship. Every result is precomputed and local; the voyage never blocks while the pipelines run.

F1 – F6

The six instruments

Numbered the way the codebase numbers them: each is a pipeline, a scene, and a model that had to earn its place.

F1 · Star Atlas

Semantic search

A phrase becomes a constellation.

A natural-language query embeds through CLIP; the collection re-forms as a sky where the most relevant works are brightest, largest and most central. If the backend is down it falls back to local lexical ranking, so the scene never dies.

Model · art-domain CLIP, contrastively fine-tuned on WikiArt captions
Zero-shot style 25.8% → 55.3%

F2 · Logbook

Style, genre, artist

Every unnamed canvas gets a page.

27 styles, 10 genres and a top-25 artist head, each with a confidence band and art-historical “echoes” of the near-miss classes — because a confident wrong answer is worse than a hedged one.

Model · multi-task ViT-L/14 @ 336, three heads, masked joint loss
Style 77.0% · genre 86.3% · artist 97.3%

F3 · Creativity Currents

Originality over time

How far a work sits from its moment.

Each painting gets a creativity reading decomposed into colour, composition and subject, plotted along the archive's own chronology — derived from the F5 map rather than asserted.

Derived client-side from the history map

F4 · Influence Routes

Directed visual lineage

Who could have seen whom.

Time-directed links between visually similar works, older to newer, with the supporting pair shown side by side so the claim can be checked by eye instead of taken on faith.

Derived client-side from the history map

F5 · Chart Table

The map of art history

Five centuries, laid out by visual kinship.

Embeddings to PCA to clustering to nearest-neighbour linking. Works with no date are dated by a trained year head before they take a place on the timeline — and works it cannot date stay visibly undated rather than being guessed.

Model · ArcFace style embedding + a year regressor over CLIP
Style p@1 0.416 → 0.610 · dating MAE 31 years

F6 · Captain's Quarters

Four classical-CV filters

No neural network answers these.

A lay figure for pose, a dye board for colour families, a line board for edge direction and a carved head for facial landmarks. Filters compose, and nothing infers at view time — every reading was computed once, in advance.

MediaPipe pose · k-means HSL · Canny + Hough · RetinaFace

The models are downloadable

How it fits together

React Three Fiber over Flask over MongoDB

The frontend is a set of 3D scenes; the backend is a Flask API with six self-contained pipeline directories; everything heavy runs as a background job so the voyage stays interactive while models work.
